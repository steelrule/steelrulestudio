The calls you never knew you missed

Five people cannot always reach the phone, and a caller who hangs up leaves no trace. This answers when nobody can, works out whether it is a job worth taking, books it, and writes down every call either way.

"We don't know what calls we're missing. If somebody doesn't leave a message, we don't know if it was a wrong call or a prank call or a spam call, or if it was a customer who just moved on to another company. My gut says that's a bigger problem than we realise."

Joe, 15 September
