What stays the same

  • Zoho FSM is still the record. This writes into it instead of asking someone to.
  • TeamUp stays until you decide otherwise. The entry lands there without the retyping.
  • Dave assigns the technician. The booking arrives unassigned, on his board.
  • Chris can still book by hand. It shows up the same way, once.
  • The customer can always call. The line in item one answers when nobody can.

The rules it follows

  • It only offers windows that are open, inside your hours. Never a made-up time.
  • It never diagnoses. The app says the system needs a look; a person says what is wrong.
  • Anything unusual goes to a person: a roof leak, a commercial site, an upset customer.
  • When your team spots it first, a person looks before anything is sent.
  • You can pause it any time, and requests go back to the queue as they are today.
