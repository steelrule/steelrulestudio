Photos and notes go straight onto the work order.

Take photo opens the camera from the job. Choose from library picks the ones already on the phone. Each photo is labeled for what it shows: the array, the inverter, the nameplate with model and serial, the site.

Photos upload in the background and retry on their own, so a weak signal on a roof does not lose them. Notes can be typed or dictated.

The warranty part is where the money is. Because the app knew the inverter was covered, this is an RMA to SolarEdge and a labor bill, instead of a part you eat or pass on.

A job is not done until the record is.

A tech closes a job by completing this, and completion is what releases the pay for it. Four taps.

Completion is what marks the job payable. No photos, no notes, no closed job. Which means no more chasing anyone for them.

What this means for you

  • Zoho FSM stays the system of record. This is a phone screen on top of it, not a replacement for it. Everything the tech does lands in the work order.
  • It works on a phone, on a roof, with bad signal. Today's jobs, yesterday's unfinished one, the customer, the system, the warranty. Nothing else.
  • Documentation becomes the gate to getting paid, which is the thing that makes it happen every time.
  • A new tech starts with a text. The link opens to their name and today's jobs. No app store, no training session.
