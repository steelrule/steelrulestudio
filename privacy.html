Steel Rule Desk: privacy

Steel Rule Desk is an internal tool used only by the two partners of Steel Rule Studio to keep track of our own business conversations. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it reads. With our own permission, it connects to our own Gmail inboxes. It reads business threads (messages with our clients, prospects and each other) and leaves personal mail alone. It sends only messages one of us has read and approved.
What it does with it. It keeps copies of business threads in our private records so we never miss a reply. To draft a reply for us to review, message text may be processed by our AI model provider. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.
Where it lives. On our own server and in our private records, open only to the two of us.
Limited Use. Steel Rule Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
Your choices. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Questions: hello@steelrulestudio.com.
